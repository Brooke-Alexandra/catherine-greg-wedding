Why Salina

Some places simply capture your heart. For us, Salina is one of those places.

Tucked away in the Aeolian Islands off the coast of Sicily, Salina has a magic all its own. It’s a place where life seems to slow down, where the water is impossibly clear, the landscape impossibly green, and the days are best spent exploring winding island roads on a scooter, discovering hidden coves, and stopping wherever the view takes your breath away.

It’s the scent of the sea, the endless green hills, the caper bushes growing wild along the roadsides, long lunches, beautiful local wines, and food that is both wonderfully simple and unforgettable. It’s the understated elegance of the island, the warmth of its people, and a way of life that reminds you that the best things are often the simplest.

There is something incredibly special about Salina. It is effortlessly beautiful, unpretentious, and full of life. A little wild, a little romantic, and entirely its own.

We chose Salina because it feels like a place where memories are made. And more than anything, we wanted to bring our favorite people somewhere we love, to experience the island’s magic with us.

We hope you’ll fall in love with it, too.

Recommended Neighborhoods

Here are some fun places to explore around the island. We suggest renting scooters.

01 · 5.5 km from venue · 14 min drive

Valdichiesa

Climb Monte Fossa delle Felci, the island's signature trek and the highest summit in the Aeolian Islands. The trail winds through lush woodland to the old volcanic crater, with sweeping views from the top. Start near the Sanctuary of Valdichiesa and allow around 4–5 hours round trip, depending on your route.

02 · 8.9 km from venue · 20 min drive

Santa Marina Salina

It's a lovely place to wander, with a lively main street, charming boutiques, cafés, and a picturesque harbor. Stop for a swim at the pebble beach, then enjoy an evening stroll along the waterfront.

03 · 8.1 km from venue · 21 min drive

Pollara

This tiny seaside village sits in a dramatic volcanic crater that opens onto the sea. Come for the breathtaking views, especially at sunset, and to see the landscape featured in Il Postino.

04 · 10.1 km from venue · 24 min drive

Rinella

Rinella's laid-back fishing-village charm, dark volcanic beach, and crystal-clear water make it perfect for a relaxing swim. Stop by Pa.Pe.Rò for its famous ricotta granita with capers and enjoy the beautiful views of Vulcano.

Travel

Make a trip of it

Since you'll be traveling all the way to Sicily, we hope you'll consider extending your stay to explore more of the island before or after the wedding.

Getting to Salina from Catania Airport

It's about a 1 hour 45 minute drive to Milazzo, followed by a Liberty Lines hydrofoil to Salina that takes around 1 hour 40 minutes. A helicopter is also an option.

Getting to Salina from Palermo Airport

The port is about a 30-minute drive, and the direct Liberty Lines hydrofoil to Salina takes approximately 3 hours 15 minutes. A helicopter is not an option.

Suggested routes

Taormina / Mount Etna → Salina

Syracuse & Ortigia → Noto → Salina

Palermo → Mondello Beach → Cefalù → Salina

These routes work particularly well if you have at least 3–5 days before or after the wedding.
